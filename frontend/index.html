About Us

Zerosum is a forward-thinking engineering company dedicated to delivering world-class aerospace and industrial control system solutions to India's rapidly evolving technology ecosystem. Founded on the conviction that India deserves access to cutting-edge technologies.

Through strategic partnerships with globally recognized technology leaders, we provide advanced UAV subsystems and engineering solutions, including high-performance propulsion systems, precision parachute recovery systems, intelligent autopilots, RF communication systems, payload integration, and comprehensive drone testing and validation equipment.

Beyond supplying advanced technologies, we provide specialized technical consultancy for UAV systems, assisting organizations with system architecture and mission-specific engineering to ensure reliable and efficient platform performance.

Driven by engineering excellence, innovation, and customer success, we aim to empower the next generation of aerospace and industrial technologies.
